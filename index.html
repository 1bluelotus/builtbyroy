About

I began building this website for my dad (Roy) when, after more than twenty years working as an audio engineer at Microsoft Corporation, he finally decided to retire.

For as long as I can remember, Dad has loved to build things. The cool thing about him is that it doesn't matter what medium it is. He draws, codes, cuts, glues, folds, solders, and hammers with whatever materials he can get his hands on to build whatever cool idea happens to pop into his head that day.

Sometimes it's something small that solves a problem around the house. Other times, it's some elaborate gadget to entertain me and my brother. A lot of the time, it's some funky idea that doesn't make any sense when he describes it, but looks awesome when its finished.

For all of the crazy stuff that Dad makes, though, the most memorable are the things that he's made to help other people. If you needed something that a trip to the supermarket wouldn't fulfill and told my Dad about it, he'll stay up late for days on end tinkering with various ideas until he invents something that solves your problem.

Because of how much he loves to help his family, neighbors, and colleagues, my Dad is a real pillar in our community. He's often the first person that people go to when they need something built, fixed, or designed. Everyone knows that if you need help with something, Roy's the one who'll help you get it done, whether that's a custom wooden photo frame for a cherished family photo or a set of laser-cut polycarbonate labels for your vegetable garden.

I made this website so that everyone can see all the cool stuff that my Dad has made throughout the years, as well as all the cool stuff he'll make in the future. He's always talked about getting his work out to a wider audience, and after all he's done for me, this is the least I can do for him.
